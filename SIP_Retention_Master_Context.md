# Strategic Initiative: First-Time Investor 12-Month SIP Retention

## 1. Role & Product Context
* **Role:** Product Manager, Growth Team
* **Product Selected:** **Groww** (India's premier retail wealth-tech platform, serving 40M+ users with the highest share of first-time retail mutual fund investors).
* **Strategic Mandate:** Maximize long-term wealth compounding for new investors while driving platform LTV and steady AUM under management.

---

## 2. Core Strategic Objective & Metrics
* **Goal:** Increase the percentage of first-time mutual fund investors who maintain an **active SIP 12 months (M12) after initiating their first SIP**.
* **Active SIP Definition:** At least one successful SIP installment debited/processed within the preceding 30 days.
* **Primary North Star Metric:** 
  $$\text{12M SIP Retention Rate} = \frac{\text{First-time MF investors with } \ge 1 \text{ successful installment in Month 12}}{\text{Total first-time MF investors who initiated a SIP in Month 0}} \times 100$$
* **Key Supporting / Input Metrics:**
  * Month-over-month SIP retention curve ($M_1, M_2, M_3, M_6, M_9, M_{12}$)
  * Voluntary Cancellation / Pause Rate vs. Involuntary Drop Rate (e.g., mandate failure, insufficient funds)
  * Mean & median months to first pause or lapse
  * SIP restart / recovery rate post-lapse

---

## 3. Strict Operating Constraints
1. **Zero Fund/Stock Recommendations:** Absolutely NO recommending specific schemes, AMCs, asset classes, or stock picks (complies with SEBI RIA rules and product constraints).
2. **Zero Fear, FOMO, or Urgency Tactics:** NO notifications or UI mechanisms that rely on anxiety, panic ("Don't miss the bull run!"), guilt ("You're failing your future!"), or artificial countdown timers / false urgency. Solutions must be rooted in intrinsic motivation, friction elimination, value reinforcement, and habit architecture.

---

## 4. Final Problem Discovery Synthesis (The Dual Behavioral Engine)
First-time SIP drop-offs before Month 12 are driven by two interlocking behavioral realities:

1. **Effort-Reward Disillusionment (The Invisibility of Year-1 Compounding):**
   * The monthly sacrifice is immediate, tangible, and real (-₹2,000 every 30 days).
   * But compounding in Year 1 is mathematically imperceptible (98% of the balance is the user's own principal).
   * Cold accounting statements displaying raw NAV and ±0.5% return trigger **Expectation Disconfirmation**: the user feels their money is "doing nothing", and the SIP transforms into an unrewarding recurring expense.

2. **The Unanchored Asset Vacuum (Mental Accounting Absence):**
   * Generic mutual fund folios (`Groww Nifty 50 Index Fund`) carry zero emotional boundaries.
   * Unlike culturally sacred assets (Gold, PPF, Real Estate), unanchored mutual funds are perceived as liquid surplus cash.
   * When everyday life shocks arrive (Diwali shopping, a friend's wedding, travel, gadget purchase), this unallocated fund is the easiest, least guilt-inducing asset to raid or pause.

3. **The Lethal Interlock:**
   * When cold statements convince the user that their money isn't growing anyway, and the fund has no assigned life job, stopping or plundering it becomes the obvious path of least resistance.
