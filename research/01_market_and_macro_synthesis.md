# Market & Competitive Synthesis: The Leaky Bucket Dilemma

## 1. Macro Market Dynamics (AMFI / Industry Data - Sep 2026)
* **Total MF AUM:** ₹82.64 Lakh Cr (+125% growth vs Sep 2021).
* **SIP AUM:** ₹17.86 Lakh Cr (accounting for 21.6% of overall MF AUM).
* **Monthly Inflows:** ₹32,490 Cr/month with 10.06 Cr contributing SIP accounts.
* **Unique Investor Base:** 6.33 Cr (~4% of India’s population).
* **The "Leaky Bucket" Crisis:** **94.5% SIP Discontinuation-to-New Registration Ratio** in FY26.
  * For every 100 new SIPs registered, ~95 SIPs are discontinued, paused, or completed.
  * Aggregate monthly SIP numbers keep breaking records only because existing high-net-worth investors increase ticket sizes and fresh user acquisition remains at peak velocity.
  * Beneath the headline growth lies an aggressive churn treadmill, especially among new retail entrants.

### Strategic Implication for Groww:
Groww has won the **top of the funnel** through zero-friction onboarding, clean UI, and UPI integration. However, because Groww captures the largest share of **first-time, inexperienced mutual fund investors**, Groww is exposed to the highest rate of 12-month SIP lapses. The next phase of platform growth cannot rely on acquisition alone; it requires plugging the 12-month retention leak.

## 3. Discontinuation Anatomy: Regulatory & Operational Mechanics
1. **The 3-Strike Rule (SEBI / AMC Mandate):** If 3 consecutive SIP debits fail (due to insufficient balance or technical rejections), the AMC/RTA automatically purges and cancels the SIP mandate.
2. **The Bank Penalty Trap:** A bounced NACH/e-Mandate incurs an ECS return fee (₹250–₹500 charged by the user's bank). This triggers severe user resentment toward the platform, prompting voluntary cancellation of the mandate.
3. **Maturity vs. Voluntary Churn:** While AMFI data mixes tenure completion with cancellations, first-time investors overwhelmingly select "Until Cancelled" (Perpetual SIP), meaning almost all early drop-offs (<12 months) are pure churn.
