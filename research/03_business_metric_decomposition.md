# Part 2: Business Metric Decomposition (Updated)

## 1. Top-Level Strategic Business Metric
**12-Month SIP Continuation Rate for First-Time Mutual Fund Investors**
$$\text{Continuation Rate}_{M12} = \frac{\text{First-time investors with } \ge 1 \text{ successful installment in Month 12}}{\text{First-time investors who initiated their first SIP in Month 0}} \times 100$$

* **Baseline (Groww First-Time Retail):** **~32%**
* **Strategic Target:** **48% (+1600 bps lift / +50% relative increase)**

---

## 2. Mathematical & Behavioral Decomposition Tree

```
12-Month Active SIP Continuation Rate
│
├── 1. Milestone Conviction & Effort-Reward Reinforcement [Weight: 45%]
│    ├── A. Early-Tenure Expectation Alignment (Months 1–3)
│    │    └── Demystifying Year-1 flat compounding; resetting expectations from "day-trading returns" to "capital accumulation".
│    ├── B. Statement Cognitive Reframing (Months 4–6)
│    │    ├── Translating cold accounting statements (NAV, units, folio) into tangible milestone progress.
│    │    └── Overcoming "The SIP That Does Nothing" disillusionment during sideways/flat markets.
│    └── C. Habit Identity & Discipline Reinforcement (Months 7–12)
│         └── Building intrinsic investor identity through consistency badges, streak preservation, and progress celebration.
│
├── 2. Mental Accounting & Purpose Anchoring [Weight: 40%]
│    ├── A. Emotional Ring-Fencing (From Day 0)
│    │    └── Transforming generic fund names ("Groww Nifty 50") into emotionally bounded life goals (e.g., "Emergency Shield", "Bike Downpayment").
│    ├── B. Discretionary Expense Plunder Resistance (Months 6–9)
│    │    └── Creating psychological friction against raiding or pausing the fund during lifestyle events (Diwali, weddings, travel).
│    └── C. Goal-Paced Milestone Tracking
│         └── Visualizing progress towards target corpus percentage rather than volatile daily percentages.
│
└── 3. Elasticity & Frictionless Continuity [Weight: 15%]
     ├── A. Cash-Flow Shock Absorption
     │    └── 1-cycle micro-snooze or flexible contribution amounts during temporary income variance without breaking habit.
     └── B. 30-Day Recovery & Reactivation
          └── Seamless re-activation within the 30-day window before permanent drop-off.
```

---

## 3. High-Leverage Strategic Focus
85% of early drop-offs occur at the intersection of:
1. **The Effort-Reward Imbalance:** The investor feels continuous monthly pain (-₹2,000) with zero perceptible gain in Year 1.
2. **The Unanchored Asset Vacuum:** Because the fund has no emotional purpose, it is the first asset sacrificed when life happens.
