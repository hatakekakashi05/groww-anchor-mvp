I decided to put ₹3,000 every month on auto-debit the day after my salary hits. 

[00:02:15] INTERVIEWER: How has the experience been across these 11 months? Were there moments where you felt doubt or considered stopping?

[00:02:26] PRIYA: Oh, absolutely. Around Month 4, the market went through a correction. I remember opening Groww and seeing that my ₹12,000 invested was showing as ₹11,640—a 3% drop in red. My immediate gut reaction was pure panic. I thought: "I am working 10-hour days designing interfaces, and the market is just burning my hard-earned cash." I literally had the app open on the "Cancel SIP" screen.

[00:04:50] INTERVIEWER: That’s a critical moment. What stopped you from clicking Cancel?

[00:05:01] PRIYA: I called my elder brother, who has been investing for 8 years. I vented to him: "Look, my money is down, this mutual fund thing is stressful." He laughed gently and told me something that completely rewired my perspective. He said: "Priya, when your favorite apparel brand has an end-of-season 20% sale, do you stop shopping? No, you buy more. Right now, the Nifty is on sale. Your ₹3,000 is buying more units of the top 50 companies in India than it bought last month. In your first year, you are not accumulating returns—you are accumulating unit volume."

[00:07:30] INTERVIEWER: How did that shift in framing affect your mindset?

[00:07:38] PRIYA: It was like a switch flipped. Suddenly, the red number didn't feel like a penalty; it felt like a discount voucher. I closed the cancellation screen. From that day on, whenever the auto-debit went through, I checked the units accumulated rather than the daily percentage fluctuation. And having that "Freedom Fund" label in my mind meant I never treated it as spare cash for buying shoes or going out.

[00:10:15] INTERVIEWER: When you look at the Groww app interface today, does it reinforce that perspective, or do you have to do that mental math yourself?

[00:10:28] PRIYA: As a designer, I'll be honest: the app doesn't help at all. I had to create a private Notion dashboard to track my "Freedom Fund" progress and unit accumulation. Most of my friends who started SIPs didn't have an elder brother to re-frame the numbers, and they didn't set an emotional goal. Almost every single one of them stopped between Month 4 and Month 7.

[00:13:40] INTERVIEWER: If Groww natively provided that emotional anchoring and milestone perspective in the app, what impact do you think it would have on beginner investors?

[00:13:54] PRIYA: It would be revolutionary. Beginners don't understand XIRR or NAV. They understand purpose and progress. If Groww let you name your fund "Freedom Fund" and showed: "You have completed 11 of 36 months, accumulated 142 units, and built 2.5 months of living expenses"—nobody would ever pause in a panic. You give people an emotional shock absorber right inside the app.
