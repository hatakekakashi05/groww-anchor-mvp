3. TOP BEHAVIORAL CHURN VECTORS (NLP CLASSIFICATION OUTPUT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across the 14,240 signals analyzed by the Discovery Engine, the underlying churn root causes distributed as follows:

Vector 1: Effort-Reward Disillusionment (Compounding Invisibility)
- Share of Corpus: 41.2% (5,867 signals)
- Core Behavioral Pattern: Users make real monthly financial sacrifices (₹1,000 - ₹3,000) for 4 to 8 months. Upon inspecting statements displaying raw NAV and small absolute gains (e.g., ₹30 to ₹150) or slight market dips (-₹20), users conclude the investment is "stagnant" or "failing", triggering voluntary pauses.

Vector 2: The Unanchored Asset Vacuum (Discretionary Capital Raiding)
- Share of Corpus: 34.6% (4,927 signals)
- Core Behavioral Pattern: Funds named generically ("Nifty 50 Index", "Flexi Cap") carry no psychological switching costs or mental accounting ring-fencing. When lifestyle expenses arise (weddings, gadgets, festivals, travel), users liquidate mutual fund holdings first because they perceive it as idle cash.

Vector 3: Cash Flow Timing & Rigid Mandate Friction
- Share of Corpus: 18.4% (2,620 signals)
- Core Behavioral Pattern: Payday delays or unexpected expenses cause bank auto-debit failure, resulting in ₹295–₹590 bank ECS penalty fees. Outraged users cancel the mandate to avoid repeat penalties.

Vector 4: Administrative & External Noise Overload
- Share of Corpus: 5.8% (826 signals)
- Core Behavioral Pattern: Jargon-dense regulatory notices, fund manager transitions, or conflicting social media debates cause decision paralysis, leading users to pull out.

4. HUMAN PM EVALUATION & PROMPT MODEL REFINEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The discovery engine was subjected to rigorous Golden Set benchmark testing (20 hand-labeled items). Key interventions made by the Growth PM:
- Rejected literal interpretation of "market timing" comments: When retail beginners claim they are "stopping to buy the dip later", qualitative probes show this is a cognitive excuse masking loss aversion. Reclassified to Effort-Reward Disillusionment.
- Disambiguated bank account switching from cognitive overload: Mandate failures following a salary bank change were mapped to Cash Flow Friction rather than Settings confusion.
- Ensured 100% compliance with SEBI RIA regulations: The discovery engine flagged that any proposed product interventions must strictly avoid recommending specific funds or stocks, and must focus solely on milestone framing, mental accounting, and cash-flow elasticity.
